Base: built-in T-pose
What carries over and what doesn't
  • Carried over: whole-body turn and tilt, head turn, arms, forearms, hand orientation, all three joints of each finger and thumb, thighs, shins, feet.
  • Kept from the base pose: spine curve, collarbones, toes. The editor has no separate controls for these.
  • Not stored in .pmap: the figure's position in the scene, the camera and body proportions.
